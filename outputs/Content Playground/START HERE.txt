CONTENT PLAYGROUND

Open Content Playground.html in your browser. Keep the file in one location and use the same browser so your locally saved plans remain accessible.

The first month contains 12 editable starter ideas, inspired by your restaurant client. These are examples, not a migration of the April plan. For a blank monthly plan, select another month and click Fresh monthly template.

Click any card to edit its brief, image reference, script, props and shot list. Inspiration library keeps links and uploaded screenshots. Shoot day groups the shots by location. Clients & templates lets you add clients and customize monthly content counts.

Export plan offers browser Print / Save as PDF and an editable Word-compatible .doc download. The Word export is HTML-based, not .docx; Word may show a format notice when opening it.

Data lives in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data can remove plans. Use Backup regularly. Restore replaces the studio with the selected backup.

The planner works locally. Google Fonts requires an internet connection; system fonts are used offline. Reference links open the original sites. No social account connection, automated research, or publishing is included.
